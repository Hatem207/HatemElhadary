Project Overview

This Excel project provides a comprehensive analysis of Superstore sales performance, customers, orders, products, and geographic distribution. It transforms transactional retail data into business insights through interactive dashboards and key performance indicators (KPIs).

Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of sales and transaction data. Without proper analysis, it is difficult to understand sales trends, identify high-value customers, evaluate product performance, and determine which geographic markets contribute most to revenue. This project provides a centralized view of sales, customers, orders, products, and geography to support data-driven decisions.

Dashboard Pages

Overview: monthly sales trend, sales by category, top 10 products.
Sales: monthly sales, Year-over-Year comparison, growth, and sales by region.
Customers: top 10 customers, regional distribution, and purchasing behavior.
Orders: orders by month, category, and region, plus quantity per order.
Products: product and category sales and quantity analysis.
Geography: sales, customers, and quantity by state and city.

Key KPIs

Total Sales, Total Orders, Total Quantity, Total Customers, and Sales Growth % (Sales YoY % = (Current Year Sales − Previous Year Sales) ÷ Previous Year Sales).

Key Insights

Sales vary across months and years, revealing seasonal and year-over-year trends.
A relatively small group of products contributes a significant portion of total sales.
Customer sales are concentrated among a number of high-value customers.
Sales and customer distribution vary across regions, with certain states and cities contributing more strongly.
Different product categories generate different levels of sales and order volume.
