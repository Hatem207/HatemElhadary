Tableau Project

HR Analytics
Employee Attrition Dashboard

Project Overview

This Tableau dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of employee demographics, workforce structure, employee attrition, job satisfaction, compensation, career progression, and key HR risk factors. It transforms employee-level HR data into interactive dashboards that help HR teams and business leaders understand workforce trends, identify potential attrition drivers, and support data-driven retention strategies.

  • Tableau
  • Tableau Calculations
  • Data Cleaning
  • Data Analysis
  • KPI Development
  • HR Analytics

Business Problem

Employee turnover can have a significant impact on recruitment costs, productivity, employee experience, and overall business performance. HR departments need to understand who is leaving, why employees leave, and which workforce characteristics are associated with higher attrition.

Project Objective

Provide an interactive HR analytics solution to monitor employee attrition, analyze workforce characteristics, evaluate compensation and career development, and identify potential employee retention risks.

Business Questions

  • Who is leaving, and how does attrition vary by department, job role, age, and gender?
  • Which workplace factors (overtime, satisfaction, work-life balance, travel) are associated with attrition?
  • How do compensation, salary hikes, and career progression relate to retention?
  • Which workforce segments carry an elevated attrition risk?

Key KPIs

  • Total Employees
  • Total Attrition and Attrition Rate
  • Average Income
  • Average Employee Age and Average Years
  • Average Satisfaction
  • Total Working Years and Years at Company

Dashboard Overview

The project is organized into six dashboard pages, each focused on a different part of the retention story.

01. HR Executive Overview

Headline KPIs (total employees, total attrition, attrition rate, average income, average age, average satisfaction) alongside attrition broken down by department, age group, job role, and gender, with interactive workforce filters.

Purpose: Give leaders a quick snapshot of attrition before they explore the details.

02. Employee Profile

Workforce composition by age, job level, department, education field, business travel, and marital status.

Questions answered: What does the workforce look like demographically?

03. Attrition Drivers

Compares attrition against overtime, job satisfaction, environment satisfaction, work-life balance, job involvement, and relationship satisfaction.

Questions answered: Which employee characteristics and workplace factors are associated with turnover?

04. Compensation & Career

Income by job role, stock option level, years since last promotion, salary hike vs attrition, and career progression, continued with years at company, total working years, and workforce tenure analysis.

Questions answered: How do pay, promotion timing, and experience relate to retention?

05. HR Risk Analysis

Cross-dimensional views: Department × Job Role heatmap, Age Band × Overtime, Job Level × Job Satisfaction, and Distance From Home × Attrition.

Questions answered: Which workforce segments show elevated attrition risk?

06. Filters and Interactivity

Filters for department, job role, business travel, overtime, gender, and marital status, plus age group analysis, dynamic cross-filtering, and dashboard navigation.

Key Insights

  • Segment variation: Attrition varies significantly across departments and job roles.
  • Workload: Overtime is an important factor when analyzing attrition.
  • Engagement: Job satisfaction and job involvement are valuable retention indicators.
  • Pay and growth: Compensation, salary increases, career progression, and time since last promotion all shape employee experience and retention.
  • Travel and commute: Business travel level and distance from home show different attrition patterns worth monitoring.
  • Demographics: Attrition patterns differ across groups such as age and gender.
  • Combined view: Looking at satisfaction, compensation, workload, and career factors together gives a more complete picture of retention risk.

Business Value

  • Monitor overall employee attrition and identify high-risk workforce segments.
  • Understand the major factors associated with turnover.
  • Compare attrition across departments and job roles.
  • Evaluate satisfaction, compensation, and career progression.
  • Support engagement strategies, workforce planning, and data-driven HR decisions.
