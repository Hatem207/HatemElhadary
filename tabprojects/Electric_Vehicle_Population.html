Tableau Project

Electric Vehicle
Analytics Dashboard

Project Overview

This Tableau dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of electric vehicle registrations and adoption trends. It helps users explore EV growth, vehicle types, manufacturers, models, electric range, CAFV eligibility, and geographic distribution through interactive dashboards and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The dashboard is designed to provide a clear overview of the electric vehicle market and support data-driven insights into EV adoption patterns.

  • Tableau
  • Tableau Desktop
  • Data Visualization
  • Calculated Fields
  • Geographic Mapping
  • Interactive Dashboards

Business Problem

The electric vehicle market is growing rapidly, creating a need to understand adoption trends, vehicle manufacturers, popular models, geographic distribution, and vehicle characteristics. This dashboard uses electric vehicle population data to explore these questions and provide a clear overview of the market.

Business Questions

  • How many electric vehicles are registered in the analyzed dataset?
  • What percentage of vehicles are BEVs and PHEVs?
  • Which manufacturers and models are most common?
  • How has EV adoption changed across model years?
  • Which U.S. states have the highest concentration of EVs?
  • What is the CAFV eligibility distribution?
  • What is the average electric driving range?

Dashboard Pages

The Executive Dashboard brings the main electric vehicle metrics and visualizations together in one interactive view.

  • Total Vehicles
  • BEV Vehicles % and PHEV Vehicles %
  • Average Electric Range
  • Top 10 EV Makes
  • EV Growth by Model Year
  • CAFV Eligibility
  • EVs by State
  • Top 10 EV Models

01. Top 10 EV Makes

Compares manufacturers by registered vehicle count and highlights leading makes such as Tesla, Nissan, Chevrolet, Ford, BMW, Kia, Toyota, and Volkswagen.

02. EV Growth by Model Year

Shows how the number of electric vehicles changes across model years, helping users explore adoption trends, periods of growth, and recent market expansion.

03. CAFV Eligibility

Examines Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle eligibility categories: eligible, eligibility unknown, and not eligible due to low battery range.

04. Geographic Distribution

Maps EV registrations across U.S. states to reveal geographic concentration and identify areas with higher electric vehicle adoption.

05. Top 10 EV Models

Displays the most common vehicle models, including models such as Model Y, Model 3, Leaf, Model S, Bolt EV, Volt, Niro, Model X, and ID.4.

Interactive Features

  • Model Year Range Filter
  • State, Model, and Make Filters
  • Dynamic Cross-Filtering
  • Interactive Geographic Map
  • Interactive Charts and Hover Tooltips
  • Dynamic KPI Updates

Key Insights

  • The dashboard reports approximately 149,804 registered electric vehicles in the analyzed dataset.
  • Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) represent the majority of the analyzed EV population.
  • Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs) represent a smaller portion of the market.
  • Tesla is the leading EV manufacturer in the dataset by a significant margin.
  • Model Y and Model 3 are among the most popular EV models.
  • EV registrations show strong growth in more recent model years.
  • A significant portion of vehicles has an unknown CAFV eligibility status.
  • EVs are geographically concentrated in specific U.S. states.

Dataset

The project uses the Electric Vehicle Population Data dataset. Available fields include VIN, County, City, State, Postal Code, Model Year, Make, Model, Electric Vehicle Type, CAFV Eligibility, Electric Range, Base MSRP, Legislative District, Vehicle Location, Electric Utility, and Census Tract.

Business Value

  • Help automotive companies understand market competition.
  • Help manufacturers identify popular EV models.
  • Allow analysts to monitor EV adoption trends.
  • Help policy makers understand geographic EV distribution.
  • Support evaluation of EV market opportunities and areas with high adoption potential.

Dashboard Design

The dashboard uses a dark professional theme and interactive components, including KPI cards, bar charts, line charts, pie charts, geographic maps, treemaps, and filters.
