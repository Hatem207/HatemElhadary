Tableau Project

Online Retail
Sales Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview

This Tableau dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of online retail sales and customer behavior. It helps businesses monitor revenue performance, customer distribution, order activity, product performance, geographic sales, and customer revenue contribution through interactive dashboards and key performance indicators (KPIs). The project turns transactional retail data into business insights that support sales monitoring and customer-focused decision-making.

  • Tableau
  • Tableau Calculations
  • Data Cleaning
  • Data Analysis
  • KPI Development
  • Dashboard Design

Business Problem

Online retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data every day. Without proper analysis, it is hard to understand sales trends, identify high-value customers, monitor product performance, and see which countries contribute most to revenue. This dashboard gives decision-makers a centralized view of sales and customer performance.

Project Objective

Transform transactional retail data into an interactive Tableau dashboard that supports sales performance monitoring and data-driven, customer-focused decisions.

Business Questions

  • How is revenue trending over time, and is it growing or declining?
  • Which customers contribute the most revenue?
  • Which products and countries perform best?
  • What does Average Order Value say about customer purchasing behavior?

Dataset

The project uses an Online Retail transactional dataset of approximately 10,000 records with the fields InvoiceNo, StockCode, Description, Quantity, InvoiceDate, UnitPrice, CustomerID, and Country.

Key KPIs

  • Total Revenue
  • Total Orders
  • Total Quantity
  • Total Customers
  • Average Order Value (AOV)
  • Average Revenue per Customer
  • Revenue Growth %

Key Calculations

  • Revenue: Quantity × Unit Price
  • Average Order Value: Total Revenue ÷ Distinct Orders
  • Average Revenue per Customer: Total Revenue ÷ Distinct Customers
  • Revenue Growth %: (Current Revenue − Previous Revenue) ÷ Previous Revenue

Dashboard Overview

01. Executive Sales Dashboard

Total revenue, total orders, quantity sold, customer count, AOV, average revenue per customer, and revenue growth, alongside the revenue trend over time, revenue by country, top 10 products by revenue, and top customers by revenue.

Purpose: Give decision-makers a quick snapshot of overall sales performance.

02. Customer Analytics

Customer distribution by country, customer revenue distribution, revenue contribution by customer, revenue-based customer segmentation, and average revenue per customer.

Questions answered: Who are the highest-value customers, and how concentrated is revenue?

03. Product and Geographic Analysis

Top 10 products by revenue, product-level comparison and ranking, revenue by country, and identification of high-performing markets.

Questions answered: Which products and markets drive the most revenue?

04. Filters and Interactivity

Dynamic filtering and cross-filtering between visualizations, with time-based, country-level, customer-level, and product-level analysis and interactive KPI monitoring.

Business Insights

  • Trends: Revenue can be monitored across periods to identify growth and decline.
  • Customer concentration: A relatively small group of customers can contribute a significant portion of total revenue.
  • Geography: Customer distribution varies significantly across countries, and revenue by country highlights the strongest markets.
  • Products: Top-performing products offer opportunities for inventory and sales optimization.
  • Behavior: Average Order Value is an important indicator of purchasing behavior.

Business Value

  • Monitor overall sales performance and track revenue growth.
  • Identify high-value customers and analyze purchasing behavior.
  • Identify top-performing products and compare revenue across countries.
  • Support sales and marketing decisions and customer-focused strategies.
